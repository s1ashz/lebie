Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Contact: faturas.investimento@gmail.com

This Google OAuth application is a personal tool used only by Francisco Filipe to read utility invoices from his own Gmail mailbox and send monthly expense emails for his own properties.

It accesses only the Gmail account the owner authorises (gmail.readonly, gmail.send, gmail.modify). It does not collect, sell, or share other people’s data, and it is not offered as a public service.

OAuth tokens stay on the owner’s private devices/server. Invoice handling is for that personal workflow only.

To revoke access: Google Account → Third-party access.
